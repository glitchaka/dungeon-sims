Production modular geography SPRITE ATLAS for an illustrated medieval island simulation. Style reference: the attached painterly island; use only its hand-painted geological appearance, warm olive grass, angular blue-gray limestone, cream sand and turquoise shallows. Never photorealistic material swatches. Fixed coherent oblique three-quarter camera, about 35 degrees above ground, looking at the SOUTH and EAST faces; north always points upper-right and east lower-right. Consistent sun from upper-left. TRANSPARENT background. Exactly SIX distinct, completely separated terrain chunks arranged in a STRICT 3 columns x 2 rows layout, equal-size cells, generous transparent padding, every chunk entirely inside its own cell with no overlap, ground contact baseline consistent. No grid lines, captions, borders, UI, trees, vegetation props, buildings, roads, bridges or people. Each chunk is natural terrain, NOT a voxel cube, NOT a floating rectangular slab, NOT a whole island. The land top surfaces must meet the same elevation and style at shared logical diamond-grid edges, while the exposed shore follows a natural irregular curve. Terrain should form continuous land when neighbors overlap slightly; no decorative grass fringe around joining interior edges. Variation in size means some pieces occupy one diamond and others two or four neighboring diamonds. All six chunks must use identical camera and lighting. COAST CONNECTIONS SHEET. Row 1 left to right: (1) north-facing straight coastline, land south and sea north, shallow sand and rocky rim; (2) east-facing straight coastline, land west, visible tall continuous limestone cliff on the east; (3) south-facing straight coastline, land north, exposed grassy plateau ending in a wide connected eroded south cliff. Row 2 left to right: (4) west-facing straight coastline, land east, sandy interrupted rock shore; (5) convex southeastern cape with grass occupying the northwest quadrant and sea wrapping south/east, broad natural rounded shape; (6) convex southwestern cape with grass northeast and sea wrapping south/west. Coastline chunks use one equal diamond footprint; land runs flush to the join boundaries. Paint ONLY a narrow turquoise foam fringe outside the exposed shoreline, not a large ocean square. Some cliff height variation, but keep land connection height consistent.
