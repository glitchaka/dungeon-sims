Production modular geography SPRITE ATLAS for an illustrated medieval island simulation. Style reference: the attached painterly island; use only its hand-painted geological appearance, warm olive grass, angular blue-gray limestone, cream sand and turquoise shallows. Never photorealistic material swatches. Fixed coherent oblique three-quarter camera, about 35 degrees above ground, looking at the SOUTH and EAST faces; north always points upper-right and east lower-right. Consistent sun from upper-left. TRANSPARENT background. Exactly SIX distinct, completely separated terrain chunks arranged in a STRICT 3 columns x 2 rows layout, equal-size cells, generous transparent padding, every chunk entirely inside its own cell with no overlap, ground contact baseline consistent. No grid lines, captions, borders, UI, trees, vegetation props, buildings, roads, bridges or people. Each chunk is natural terrain, NOT a voxel cube, NOT a floating rectangular slab, NOT a whole island. The land top surfaces must meet the same elevation and style at shared logical diamond-grid edges, while the exposed shore follows a natural irregular curve. Terrain should form continuous land when neighbors overlap slightly; no decorative grass fringe around joining interior edges. Variation in size means some pieces occupy one diamond and others two or four neighboring diamonds. All six chunks must use identical camera and lighting. CURVED COAST CONNECTIONS SHEET. All pieces use one logical diamond footprint and connect at the same grassy elevation as the straight coast set. Row 1 left to right: (1) convex northeastern cape: grass southwest, water wraps north/east; (2) convex northwestern cape: grass southeast, water wraps north/west; (3) concave northeastern bay notch: water cuts into the NE corner, the other three corners are continuous grassy land. Row 2 left to right: (4) concave northwestern bay notch, water NW, other corners land; (5) concave southeastern bay notch, water SE, other corners land; (6) concave southwestern bay notch, water SW, other corners land. Natural broad limestone coastline and sandy low coves, not repeated little rock props. No paths, stairs or detached boulders. Narrow turquoise foam fringe only on water edges. Joining grass edges are clean continuous landscape, not isolated islands.
